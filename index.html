How I think about marketing

Great marketing rarely happens in silos. I connect brand, performance marketing, partnerships, operations, and customer experience so they work as one system instead of separate projects. Whether I'm launching a campaign, leading a cross-functional initiative, or figuring out why performance shifted, I'm happiest solving complex problems alongside great people. I let data guide the decisions, and I never lose sight of the story, or the people, behind the numbers.

I've led marketing for members, consumers, and causes: a national food industry association, a seven-brand fitness and wellness portfolio, and a national nonprofit. That work spans brand architecture, integrated campaigns, paid acquisition, CRM and member data, events, partnerships, and community.
